# PostBox Privacy Policy (draft)

*Last updated: <date of publication>. Publisher: Uniqueone. Contact: <contact email>.*

PostBox is an API client that runs entirely on your device.

**What we collect:** nothing. PostBox has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no cloud sync. We (Uniqueone) receive no data from the app.

**What the app stores on your device:** your collections, saved requests, environments, request history and settings (in the app's private storage), and your secrets such as passwords, tokens and secret variables (in the operating system's secure storage, for example Android Keystore, Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service). You can delete all of it at any time under Settings > Data and privacy > Clear local data.

**Network traffic:** PostBox connects only to the servers you enter in requests, and to the sign-in pages of OAuth providers you configure. Those servers have their own privacy policies. If you use OAuth, PostBox opens your browser and receives the response on a local address (`127.0.0.1` on desktop, an app link on Android).

**Sharing and exports:** exports leave secrets out unless you choose to include them. Anything you export or copy is under your control.

**Children:** PostBox is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

**Changes:** changes to this policy will be published with a new date.

This is a draft for the publisher to review (and have reviewed legally) before publication.
